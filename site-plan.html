Exoplanet Explorer

A working plan for the individual final project: a dynamic site for browsing confirmed exoplanets using real NASA data.

Site Name

Exoplanet Explorer

The name states plainly what the site does: it lets a visitor explore planets outside our solar system. It's short, easy to say out loud, and reads clearly as a space/science tool rather than a blog or store, which matches the site's purpose of presenting real astronomical data.

Site Purpose

Exoplanet Explorer gives visitors an approachable way to browse confirmed exoplanets discovered by NASA missions. The site lists planets with key details — size, distance from Earth, and discovery year — and lets a visitor open any planet in a modal for a closer look. A saved favorites list, stored in the browser via local storage, lets a returning visitor pick up where they left off without creating an account.

Scenarios

Questions a visitor from the target audience — space enthusiasts, students, and amateur astronomers — might bring to the site:

  • Which confirmed exoplanets are closest to Earth, and how far away are they in light-years?
  • How do I save a planet I find interesting so I can find it again later without searching from scratch?

Color Scheme

Three colors make up the palette. This site plan document uses only these colors.

Space Navy — #0B0E1A Primary background across the site, evoking the darkness of space.
Starlight Cyan — #4FD1FF Headings, links, and interactive elements like buttons and the modal trigger.
Exoplanet Gold — #FFB86B Small accents and highlights: favorited items, badges, and the eyebrow label.

Typography

Two fonts are selected for the site.

Headings — Space Grotesk

Kepler-186f

Used for all headings (h1–h3), the site logo, and card titles. Its geometric, slightly technical feel matches a data-driven science tool.

Body Text — Inter

Used for paragraphs, list items, form labels, and modal descriptions. Inter stays highly readable at small sizes, which matters since planet detail cards pack in several numeric data points.
